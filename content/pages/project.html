Hospitality

Providing a tactile anchor for the world's most serene retreats, where the silence of the loom meets the luxury of the hotel.

01 / Story

Our hospitality projects begin with a deep study of the site's spirit, treating the floor as the fifth wall of an architectural masterpiece. We believe in a silent dialogue between the structure and the surface; at Aman New York, this manifested as a mirroring of the meditative atmosphere of the Ganges. By translating the rhythmic ebb and flow of sacred waters into muted, rhythmic textures, we ground the guest experience in a sense of place that is both ancient and profoundly modern.

02 / Process

The creation of a hospitality centerpiece is a marathon of meditative focus. Using 150-knot precision, our master weavers follow the rhythmic talim - the oral song of the pattern - spending over 2,000 hours on a single lobby rug. This extreme density is not merely for detail; it creates a structural integrity designed to withstand the soft but constant footfall of global travelers, ensuring the rug's luster remains unchanged for decades of service.

03 / Materials

We source our Highland wool from flocks grazing at altitudes above 4,000 meters, where the cold produces a fiber rich in natural lanolin. This is blended with hand-reeled Mulberry silk, then dyed in small batches using organic madder root and indigo. The result is a living color - a depth of hue that shifts and dances across the silk threads as the light moves through a hotel lobby from dawn to dusk.
