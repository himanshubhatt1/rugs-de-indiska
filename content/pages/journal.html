01 — Craft

The Geometry of
Silence

"Every rug begins as a silent map."

Before the first thread is dyed, a master designer spends months hand-drawing the "Naksha" - a mathematical blueprint where every cell represents a singular knot. This grid is the soul of the final work, translated by the rhythmic movement of fingers across the loom.
