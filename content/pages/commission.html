Bespoke Rug Commission

Crafted Exclusively for Your Space

From the first conversation to the final handcrafted masterpiece, we collaborate with you to create a rug that reflects your vision, your space, and your story.

Phase I

Private Consultation

The journey begins with an intimate dialogue. We seek to understand not just the dimensions of your room, but the atmosphere you wish to curate.

In this sanctuary of design, we explore your aesthetic preferences, architectural context, and personal narratives. Our design directors guide you through the infinite possibilities of fiber, knot, and color.

"A rug is the foundation of a sanctuary, a quiet anchor for life's most cherished moments."

Phase II

Design Development

Translating abstract thought into technical precision. Our studio in Bhadohi breathes life into your bespoke vision through iterative artistry.

I. The Naksha

A hand-painted architectural graph that serves as the weaver's language. Every knot is accounted for before the loom is even threaded.

II. Chromatic Harmonies

Custom dye batches are created to match your palette exactly. We offer a library of over 3,000 poms, or create new pigments for unique projects.

III. The Looming

A labor of patience. In Bhadohi, master weavers knot up to 100,000 knots per square meter, a process that can take six months to a year.
