The Origin

Decades of Quiet
Dedication

For three generations, our family has practiced the slow, deliberate art of rug-making in the heart of Bhadohi. What began as a single loom in a sun-drenched atelier in Bhadohi has grown into a global beacon for heritage craftsmanship.

We do not measure our growth in metrics, but in the longevity of our pieces. A Rugs De Indiska creation is designed to outlive the room it inhabits, becoming a silent witness to a family's history.

The Founder's Vision

Shri Ramji

A Legacy of Knots and Stories

Founded by Shri Ramji in 1962, Rugs De Indiska was born from a singular passion: to preserve the soul of Indian weaving while bringing it to the world's grandest rooms.

"A rug is not just a floor covering. It is a story told in knots, a piece of earth brought into the home."

His philosophy remains our anchor, guiding every hand-knotted masterpiece that leaves our atelier today.

04 — Social Responsibility

The Apprentice Fund

Through our "Knot School" initiative, we ensure the survival of our craft. We sponsor 40 apprentices annually, providing them with a curriculum that balances traditional manual dexterity with modern design software and financial literacy.

  • Preserving the Asli-Bhadohi hand-drawing technique.
  • Digital mapping and color theory workshops.
  • Full educational scholarships for weavers' children.
